### **A resume is only part of the picture.**

Marketing roles are increasingly specialised. A candidate's experience needs to be considered alongside the responsibilities of the role, the capabilities required and the business they will work in.

Kalaidoskope looks beyond the resume to understand that fit, helping you assess candidates in the context of the work they need to do.

### **Marketing recruitment with marketing expertise.**

Our approach draws on the practical marketing experience of Laukika Consultancy Solutions. That understanding helps us look at both sides of a hiring requirement: what the business needs and what the candidate can bring.

We consider five connected factors:

| Factor | What we look at |
|---|---|
| Role | The responsibilities and expectations of the position |
| Capability | The marketing skills needed to carry out the work |
| Experience | The candidate's relevant background |
| Context | The business and team the candidate will join |
| Fit | How the candidate aligns with the hiring requirement |

### **From sourcing to preliminary interviews.**

**Candidate sourcing** — We source candidates for the marketing role you need to fill.

**Profile screening** — We review profiles against the role, considering capability, experience and business context.

**Preliminary interviews** — We conduct initial interviews to understand candidates beyond their resumes and assess their suitability for the requirement.

**Candidate shortlisting** — We bring forward candidates aligned with the role for your consideration.
