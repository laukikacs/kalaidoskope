**QUESTIONS & ANSWERS**

**A few things to know before you get started.**

### **01 / What does "AI First Marketing" mean at Kalaidoskope?**

It means understanding the customer, market, business and strategy first, then using AI where it can improve how marketing work is done. Human judgment remains central to the decisions.

### **02 / Who is the Academy programme for?**

The Academy is relevant to students, marketing professionals, managers, entrepreneurs and consultants who want to develop practical marketing capability and learn how AI can support execution.

### **03 / What does Kalaidoskope Talent help organisations with?**

We help organisations clarify the marketing role and capabilities they need, then source and assess talent against that context.

### **04 / What kind of work can Enterprise AI Tools support?**

Our focus includes recurring marketing work such as research, data collection, reporting, monitoring, briefs, documentation and content workflows. The right application depends on the team and its process.

### **05 / How can I get started?**

Join the Academy or agency product waitlist if those offerings are relevant to you. For marketing hiring or a broader conversation, contact the Kalaidoskope team.
