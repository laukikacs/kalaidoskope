**THE MARKETING ACCELERATION PROGRAM**

**Learn marketing as a connected discipline, not a collection of individual tools.**

Marketing is not limited to social media, SEO, advertising, content or AI. These are individual parts of a much larger marketing system.

Effective marketing starts by understanding what the business needs to achieve, who it needs to reach and what role marketing should play. From there, strategic decisions shape the direction of the work, while briefs and execution turn that direction into marketing activity.

The Marketing Acceleration Program is built around this connected approach.

Across four months, the programme combines structured coaching with practical application and a dedicated period of training and hands-on experience, helping learners develop a broader understanding of how marketing decisions are made and applied.

**THE KALAIDOSKOPE MARKETING FRAMEWORK**

**A structured way to think about marketing before choosing the tools.**

The framework gives you a starting point for approaching a marketing problem. Instead of immediately choosing a platform or tactic, it establishes the questions that need to be understood first.

**01 / BUSINESS**

**What does the business need to achieve?**

Start by understanding the business objective and the role marketing needs to play in reaching it.

**02 / MARKET**

**What is happening around the business?**

Understand the market, competition, opportunities and broader context that influence the brand and its marketing decisions.

**03 / CUSTOMER**

**Who does the business need to reach?**

Explore the people behind the market, including their needs, motivations, behaviour and decision-making.

**04 / STRATEGY**

**What should marketing do?**

Bring the business objective, market context and customer understanding together to establish a clear marketing direction.

**05 / BRIEF**

**What needs to be communicated or delivered?**

Translate strategic thinking into a clear requirement that can guide creative, content and marketing execution.

**06 / EXECUTION**

**How does the marketing work come to life?**

Turn the direction into actual marketing activity and consider how the resulting work can be evaluated and improved.

**BUSINESS → MARKET → CUSTOMER → STRATEGY → BRIEF → EXECUTION**

This framework establishes the **questions and thinking that sit behind marketing decisions**.

The curriculum then develops the knowledge required to work through those decisions.

**THE 4-MONTH MARKETING JOURNEY**

MAP combines **3 months of coaching with 1 month of training and hands-on experience**.

The first three months are organised around the stages through which customers typically move:

**AWARENESS → CONSIDERATION → PURCHASE & LOYALTY**

The fourth month changes the learning environment, allowing learners to experience how marketing understanding is applied in practice.

**MONTH 01 / AWARENESS**

**Build the foundation for understanding how brands become relevant.**

The first month establishes the marketing fundamentals needed to understand a business, its market and its customers.

The focus is on developing the context required before moving into more specialised marketing activity.

**MONTH 02 / CONSIDERATION**

**Understand what influences customer evaluation and choice.**

The second month moves into customer journeys, content strategy, marketing strategy, SEO, PPC and marketing briefs.

The focus is on understanding how marketing supports customers as they evaluate a brand and its offering.

**MONTH 03 / PURCHASE & LOYALTY**

**Explore marketing beyond the point of consideration.**

The third month examines purchase, engagement, digital marketing, retention and loyalty.

The focus shifts towards how marketing can support action, maintain relevance and contribute to longer-term customer relationships.

**MONTH 04 / TRAINING & HANDS-ON EXPERIENCE**

**Take your learning into a real-world marketing environment.**

The fourth month moves beyond structured coaching into training and hands-on experience.
