## **About Kalaidoskope**

### **Building the AI First Marketing Organisation.**

Kalaidoskope brings together marketing education, talent recruitment and enterprise AI systems. We help people develop practical marketing capability, organisations find the right talent and teams reduce time spent on repetitive work.

Our three connected verticals, Kalaidoskope Academy, Kalaidoskope Talent and Kalaidoskope Enterprise AI Tools, support this ambition.

### **Powered by Laukika.**

Kalaidoskope draws on the practical marketing experience of Laukika Consultancy Solutions across fintech, consumables, technology and other business categories.

That experience helps us understand where marketing teams spend their time, what capabilities the work requires and which recurring tasks can benefit from automation. We bring that understanding into how we teach, recruit and build AI systems.

### **Our mission**

To make marketing clearer to learn and more effective to practise.

We aim to develop capable marketers, connect them with organisations where their skills fit, and help teams automate repetitive tasks so they can give more attention to strategy and decisions.

### **Our vision**

To give marketing teams more time for work that creates business value.

We see competent people, thoughtful recruitment and practical AI systems working together to make this possible. Our ambition is to help marketing demonstrate its contribution to business growth and be recognised as an investment.

### **Better marketing begins with better thinking.**

We believe AI should amplify marketing thinking. Understanding the customer, market, business and strategy remains essential to deciding what to do and how to execute it.

Our approach starts with those decisions, with a clear understanding of what to automate and what should remain human.

### **Learn. Build. Automate.**

**Kalaidoskope Academy**

**Those Who Do, Now Teach.**

Our four-month programme progresses from marketing fundamentals to AI-enabled execution. Participants learn to understand businesses and customers, develop strategies and briefs, and build AI agents and marketing workflows.

**Kalaidoskope Talent**

**Marketing recruitment with marketing expertise.**

We help organisations identify the capabilities they need and find people suited to those roles. Our approach considers capability, experience, business context and fit, looking beyond the resume.

**Kalaidoskope Enterprise AI Tools**

**Automate the work. Focus on the signals.**

We are building systems to automate or accelerate research, reporting, brief creation, documentation and content workflows for marketing teams across organisations.
