# Kalaidoskope — Brand Notes (from Brand Guidelines PDF, 38pp)

Spelling: **kalaidoskope** (wordmark, all lowercase; the "ai" is set in Gold). Guide text inconsistently writes Kalaidoscope/Kaleidoscope — use "Kalaidoskope". Domain: kalaidoskope.com. Company line in samples: "Kaleidoscope AI Labs", Bengaluru.

Positioning: AI + education brand. Values: Sophistication · Authority · Leadership. Logo themes: intelligence, perspective, discovery, innovation.

## Logo
- Symbol: four-point concave star (directional form) + 3 triangles at core (viewpoints converging). Wordmark: elegant serif, lowercase.
- Versions: full colour (blue + gold "ai"), black, reversed white on Blue, reversed on Moonlight Indigo (#181a53), one-colour.
- Logomark alone = favicon / app icon / avatar (white star on Blue rounded square).
- Never stretch / rotate / recolour; keep clear space. Partner lockups: equal visual weight, 2X spacing.

## Colour
Primary: Kalaidoskope Blue #2E3192 · Kalaidoskope Gold #CC9939
Secondary: Moonlight Indigo #181A53 · Periwinkle #6B72C6 · Mist #E5E6F9
Usage pattern: white or Blue grounds; gold for accent words (e.g. first word of titles "Brand / **Guidelines**"), thin gold rules, footers.

## Type
Primary: **Timeless** (serif) Regular + Bold — headlines, wordmark feel. (Commercial; web fallback needed — closest Google: e.g. "Spectral"/"Cormorant"/"Libre Caslon"? confirm)
Secondary: **IBM Plex Sans** Regular + Bold — body, UI.
Title style: two-line title, line 1 Gold Regular, line 2 Blue Bold.

## Graphic language
- Huge cropped concave-star arcs bleeding off page edge (thin blue strokes, often multiple parallel lines blue + gold).
- Pattern: grid of small outlined star marks.
- Large cropped triangles in Blue/Gold.
- Photo frames: arch/circle cutouts with gold ring, shapes cut by star curve. Imagery: cinematic, warm, moody portraits & students.
- Thin outline icon set (single weight).
- Layout chrome: wordmark bottom-left, "Page N" bottom-right, gold hairline above footer.
- Social refs: dark, bold, high-energy tech posters (contrast to the calm core system).
